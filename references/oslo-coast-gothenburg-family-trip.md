# Oslo → Bohuslän coast → Gothenburg family road trip (researched Oct 2026)

Family: Florian + wife, daughters Lilia (12) and Flora (9). Driving a Volvo,
Oslo → Gothenburg. Split requested: 2 nights Oslo, 2 nights on the coast,
3 nights Gothenburg. Dates: Sat 24 Oct – Sat 31 Oct 2026.

## Route facts
- Oslo → Gothenburg: ~295 km on the E6, ~3.5 h driving. Border at Svinesund
  (suspension bridge over the Idefjord, toll applies). High motorway both sides.
- Bohuslän coast (Smögen, Fjällbacka, Grebbestad) lies just west of the E6,
  roughly halfway — a natural mid-drive stop, adds ~30–45 min total.
- October weather: 5–10 °C, grey, some rain. ~9 h daylight. Plan indoor backup.
- Sweden ~15% cheaper than Norway on the official price index (Destatis 2025).

## October seasonality caveat (west coast)
- Villages stay open (Smögenbryggan boardwalk, Fjällbacka harbour) — free,
  atmospheric, quiet off-season.
- Many cafés, restaurants, and boat tours CLOSE after summer. Lobster season
  runs from late Sept, so some seafood spots stay open.
- Hotels: some year-round (TanumStrand, Grebbestad Fjordens Camping), many
  guesthouses close. Verify year-round status before booking.

## Verified hotels (via curl of own-sites, Oct 2026)

### Coast — TanumStrand SPA & Resort (Grebbestad) ✅ year-round
- Open year-round; runs fall/winter packages (Autumn holiday, Archipelago,
  Lobster fishing) so late Oct is in-season.
- Family room fits 2+2 exactly: 22 m², one bedroom + living room with sofa bed
  + sleeping alcove with bunk bed + two bathrooms.
- ~11 min from the Tanum rock carvings (UNESCO, free, open 24/7) + Vitlycke
  Museum (only café in the area).
- Site: tanumstrand.se/en/hotell/

### Gothenburg — Liseberg Grand Curiosa ✅
- Grand Curiosa room: 29 m², up to 5 guests — fits family of 4.
- Halloween package: from SEK 895/person (for 4) — accommodation + Liseberg
  admission + Ride Pass. Smart buy since trip is during Halloween season
  (Liseberg Halloween runs 2 Oct–1 Nov).
- Also: Hotel & Dinner from SEK 1,245/person; dinner-buffet package from
  SEK 625/person.
- Upgrades: Superior Curiosa 44 m²/6 guests; Deluxe 82 m²/7 guests.
- Site: liseberg.se/en/liseberg-grand-curiosa-hotel/ (rooms page:
  .../our-rooms/)

### Oslo — Thon Hotel Rosenkrantz ⚠️
- Central (a few streets off Karl Johans gate), modern design, well-rated for
  families. Could NOT pull a live price — Thon booking is a JS widget, no
  static price in HTML. Needs browser or user click-through for exact rate.

## Recommended split
Oslo 2 → Coast 1–2 → Gothenburg 3–4. The drive is a mid-trip breather, not a
bookend. Coast stop worth it only if you want the skärgården properly (Smögen
boardwalk + Fjällbacka + Tanum carvings); otherwise it's a long-lunch stop.
